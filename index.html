A little about me

I'm a high-school student in Lebanon, currently preparing for the French Baccalauréat.

I have a lot of different interests, and I'm still figuring out which direction I want to take in the future.

I've been getting into programming and web development, building small projects and learning how things work along the way.

At the same time, I've always been interested in science and medicine, especially oncology. What interests me most is the process of connecting the dots — looking at different pieces of information, analyzing them, and trying to understand how they fit together. That's also part of what draws me to biology in general.

I'm also interested in writing, design, technology, and a few other things that don't necessarily fit into one category.

A lot of what I do comes from simply seeing something that could be improved and wanting to do something about it.

Whether it's a school project, organizing something, writing, or building something with code, I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into something real.

I don't have my whole future figured out yet, and I'm genuinely okay with that.

For now, I'm focused on learning, trying different things, and seeing where they take me.
